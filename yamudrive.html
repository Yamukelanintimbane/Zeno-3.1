About Yamudrive

The story behind our educational initiative

Our Mission

Yamudrive was created to bridge the educational resource gap for South African matriculants. Our platform aimed to provide free, high-quality exam preparation materials to students across all provinces, regardless of their socioeconomic background.

2020
Founded by Yamukelani Ntimbane with the vision of democratizing exam resources
2021
Platform discontinuation due to funding challenges

Though Yamudrive is no longer active, we're proud of the impact we made during our operation, helping thousands of students prepare for their matric examinations.
