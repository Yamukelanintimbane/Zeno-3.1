Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our app

Is the ZenoLaunch app free to download?
Yes, the ZenoLaunch app is completely free to download from the App Store and Google Play. It includes basic features at no cost. Premium features are available through optional in-app subscriptions.
What devices are supported?
Our app is available for both iOS (iPhone and iPad running iOS 13 or later) and Android (devices running Android 8.0 or later). We also have a web version that works on all modern browsers.
Does the app work offline?
No, Unfortunately, it does not.
How secure is my data?
We take security very seriously. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. Our servers are located in secure data centers in South Africa, ensuring compliance with local data protection laws.
Can I use the app for team collaboration?
Absolutely! The app includes team management features that allow you to invite team members, assign tasks, track progress, and communicate through built-in chat and video calling features.
